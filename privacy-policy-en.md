# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: July 10, 2026

---

## Introduction

CatGotcha ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

**The App has no account system, operates no backend server, and never uploads any of your data to the cloud.**

The following data, generated as you use the App, is stored exclusively on your own device:

- **Photos**: Photos you take or select to record cats you've encountered
- **Location Data**: Geographic location you authorize the App to capture when logging where you saw a cat
- **Cat & Bond Records**: The cat profiles, encounter counts, bond levels, and related progression data you create

## Data Storage & Usage

- **Local Storage Only**: All of the above data is saved using on-device local storage mechanisms and never transmitted over the network
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not offer any form of cloud backup or cross-device synchronization
- **Precise Location Is Never Made Public**: A cat's precise coordinates are used only for on-device recording and display, and are never uploaded, shared, or exposed publicly
- **Shared Content Is Automatically Sanitized**: When you share or export content such as a bond card, the App automatically strips all EXIF metadata (including GPS coordinates) from the image. Shared images only display a coarse, sanitized location label (city-level only; district-level and finer details, including landmarks, are never shown) — never information precise enough to locate a specific address or exact coordinates
- **No Photo Library Access for Picking**: When selecting a photo from your library, the App uses the system's out-of-process photo picker — the App itself is never granted read access to your photo library, and only receives the single photo you actively selected
- **Save Requires One-Time Authorization**: If you choose to save a generated share image to your system photo library, this triggers a one-time write-only permission prompt used solely for that save action

## Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions, each used only to support its corresponding feature:

- **Camera**: To take photos of cats you encounter
- **Location (While Using the App)**: To record where you encountered a cat (stored locally only; automatically sanitized before sharing)
- **Photo Library Add (One-Time)**: Triggered only when you actively choose to save a shared image to your system photo library

We never collect your location in the background, and we never read any photos in your library beyond the one you actively select.

## Third-Party Services & Data Transmission

**During the current TestFlight testing stage, the App does not integrate any third-party data collection, analytics, or advertising services.**

The App may communicate with external services over standard OS-level HTTPS connections (for example, optional services that may be integrated in future versions). These connections use only the operating system's built-in standard encryption and involve no proprietary or custom cryptographic algorithms. TestFlight-stage testing data (such as crash logs and screenshot feedback you voluntarily submit) is handled per Apple's standard TestFlight data-processing practices — see [Apple's TestFlight documentation](https://developer.apple.com/testflight/) for details.

## Data Security & Your Control

Because all data stays on your own device, you retain full control over your information:

- Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data (photos, location records, cat and bond data)
- The App does not maintain any server-side data, so there is no "delete my cloud data" request to make — no such cloud data exists

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no account system and does not collect personally identifiable information from users of any age.
